---
name: Light-surface accent text
description: Why decorative brand gold and small text on white need separate contrast treatment.
---
Preserve the decorative brand accent and button pairing when correcting small accent text on light surfaces. Treat fixed-white form surfaces separately from the surrounding theme.

**Why:** The default runtime gold is intentionally light for decoration and dark-background branding, but does not meet normal-text contrast on white. Globally darkening it would unnecessarily alter the established brand and button appearance.

**How to apply:** Use a separately evaluated text shade for light-surface labels. Check runtime scheme overrides as well as base CSS, and check both focused and populated floating-label states.
